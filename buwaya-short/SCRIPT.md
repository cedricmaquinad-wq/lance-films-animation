# The Other Buwaya (Ang Tunay na Buwaya)

Vertical cartoon short, ~56-60 s, style: 2D cartoon characters in a colourful 3D world (cartoon-3d-short skill).
Hook: the Philippines caught the world's biggest crocodile, but not the hungriest one.

## Script (English) - ~207 words, ~56 s

| Beat | Voice-over | Shot idea |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | The biggest crocodile ever caught alive came from the Philippines. And he wasn't even the hungriest one. | Huge croc eye opens in a misty swamp, push-in. White flash. |
| Setting | 2011. Bunawan, Agusan del Sur. Villagers trapped Lolong: six point one seven meters, over a thousand kilos. A world record. | Golden-hour swamp village, villagers heave a rope, Lolong in a steel cage, someone holds up a tape measure that keeps unrolling. |
| Turn | But Filipinos already had a word for a different crocodile. Buwaya. The kind that wears a barong. | Cut to a city hall: a crocodile-headed official in a barong turns to camera, smiles. |
| Escalation 1 | From 2022 to 2025, the government handed out flood control projects worth five hundred forty-five billion pesos. Almost ten thousand of them. | Aerial over a flooded town, banners and ribbon-cuttings everywhere. |
| Escalation 2 | One out of every five pesos went to just fifteen contractors. | Five piles of coins; one pile slides away to a tiny table of 15 smiling contractors. |
| Escalation 3 | In Pandi, Bulacan, a ninety-two million peso flood wall, one hundred twenty-eight meters long, was marked ninety-five percent complete. In twenty-one days. | A clipboard gets a giant "95%" stamp. Calendar pages fly. |
| Escalation 4 | So the court went to see it. | Judges in robes walk down a riverbank path, glide camera. |
| Snap | They found grass. And a few rusty steel sheets. | Wide: empty, overgrown riverbank. A single rusty sheet pile falls over (KICK). |
| Deadpan | Luckily, grass is very low maintenance. Unluckily, water goes right through it. | Close-up of grass, then a flood wave rolls through it. |
| Analogy | It's like giving your cousin a hundred pesos for halo-halo. He comes back with a receipt, a photo of a glass, and says the ice melted. Then asks for another hundred. | Sari-sari store, cousin hands over a receipt and a photo, kid stares. |
| Consequence | Up to four hundred seventy-two projects have been flagged as possible ghosts. Engineers suspended. Cases filed. And the President said it on live TV: Mahiya naman kayo. | Ghost-sheet flood walls floating over a map. TV screen in a carinderia. |
| Callback | Lolong needed a steel trap and a whole village to catch. The other buwaya don't hide in swamps. They're on the ballot. Every three years. | Lolong in his cage looks over at a campaign poster of the barong crocodile, which winks. |
